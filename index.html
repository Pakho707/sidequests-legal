How it works

No to-do list, no streak to feed. A deck of quests, and the memories you bring back.

STEP 1

Draw a quest

Swipe right to keep it, left to skip. Or browse all 318, from Easy to Legendary.

STEP 2

Do it for real

Then tap Complete: the date, the place, who was there, your photos and the story.

STEP 3

Earn aura

Every quest gives aura. Climb from NPC to Infinite Aura and unlock achievements.

Better with your crew

Add your friends with a username. Tag them in a quest, dare them to one, and see who has the most aura this week.

Your lore, your friends only

Memories are shared with your friends and nobody else, encrypted so only they can open them. Not even we can read them.

Invite in one tap

Send your invite link: whoever taps it lands on your profile, ready to join your crew.
